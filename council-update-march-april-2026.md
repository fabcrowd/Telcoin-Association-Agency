# Telcoin Association Council Updates | March–Early April 2026

Three councils met across the past several weeks with concrete deliverables: validators running in production data centers, a community rebate program progressing toward relaunch, and active infrastructure hardening ahead of mainnet.

---

## 1. Platform & Treasury Council | Testnet Soft Launch & Infrastructure Work

The Telcoin Network testnet launched in soft launch mode. This is the initial phase — a controlled rollout with roughly five MNO validators operating in bare-metal production data centers, using the same class of infrastructure that will host mainnet. This is not a dev environment. It is a wide-area network running under conditions that reflect production, and it is the most stable release to date.

The testnet serves as the long-term home for upgrade testing, developer testing, and ongoing MNO validation. A broader "Adiri" testnet phase is expected in the coming weeks, with resets planned before mainnet to incorporate final security findings and upgraded protocol versions.

The team completed a full database layer overhaul this cycle — a significant production-level upgrade that addresses a common blockchain scaling constraint. Other work this period included: improved handling of large transaction bursts (100+ tx batches), CLI tooling for validator onboarding and staking, faster node sync times, tighter epoch boundary record handling, and direct observer node support so dApp builders can pull real-time chain data without relying on third-party RPC providers.

LayerZero cross-chain bridging is in progress. Parker met with LayerZero leadership in New York to coordinate direction. MNO partners and dApp teams can run independent faucets and pay gas for testing.

Testnet documentation is being made available at tel-docs.telcoin.network. The team runs monthly coordination to align base fees, gas penalties, EVM compatibility, epoch boundaries, canonical updates, and chain growth mechanics.

Security work continues. AI-assisted log scanning is ongoing at scale, and the project is moving toward human-led audits with top EVM researchers through partners including Spearbit and Cantina.

The unified web presence project is wrapping up a 4–5 week research and UX phase. Wireframes, user journeys, competitive analysis, and audience pathways for retail users, MNOs/validators, developers, and DeFi participants are complete. The team is moving into the build phase, with delivery targeted around mainnet.

MWC Barcelona (March 2–5) generated real follow-on activity. Telcoin was among the very few blockchain projects at the event. Executive conversations with mobile operators from every continent focused on validator sign-up and payment infrastructure. Valet Digital, with over 50 million subscribers, published detailed cross-border payment discussions involving Telcoin. A GSMA advisor engagement is in its final stages.

A new Compliance Counsel has been selected. The candidate has passed the Swiss Verein model requirements, completed KYM, and signed off on all onboarding documentation.

Mainnet timing is milestone-driven. Full MNO onboarding, LayerZero bridging completion, and final security sign-off are the remaining dependencies. The roadmap and GitHub are the live sources of record.

## 2. TAN Council | TANP-1 Restart and Rising Builder Activity

TANP-1 is back. The TANP fee rebate program restarts April 26. The proposal has been reviewed and signed off. A follow-up TANP will preserve the original contract state and all lifetime staking limits.

The mechanics are straightforward. Users earn up to a full rebate on trading fees paid through the Telcoin app, capped at fees actually spent that period and distributed proportionally from a fixed pool. The program runs Wednesday-to-Wednesday. Community communications go out ahead of launch.

Two projects are generating real activity on testnet:

**tel-pets.com** is an interactive validator visualization tool — click any sponsor and see leading mobile operators, live FX rates across 41+ currencies, cheapest providers with Telcoin flagged where competitive, and a global leaderboard with key metrics breakdown.

**Tel Pets** is live in faucet mode. Users can feed a pet, complete single-transaction or burst transaction tasks, and join coordinated battle parties that simultaneously generate network load. Tel Pets earn XP and evolve through stages. With roughly 20 testers, the project has already logged over 47,000 transactions.

## 3. TELs Initiative | Merkl Trial, Balancer Holdings, Coordination

The Merkl proof-of-concept trial is in final preparation. Legal Counsel is reviewing Merkl's counter-argument on the service agreement. Integration is ready to execute — a meaningful operational upgrade shifting from weekly CSV liquidity reports to near-real-time API data (hours instead of end-of-week).

The first trial run on Balancer V4 TELs/USDC will deploy following legal sign-off — minimum of four weeks with no outbound rewards during that time. Per-position success metrics are defined. No immediate changes for existing holders. Full notice will come before any action is taken.

The Association is monitoring developments with existing Balancer pool positions. No new Balancer pools are recommended in the short term. Appropriate course of action is being evaluated.

The March 23 coordination meeting provided real clarity on the strategy moving forward — monthly recurring updates to coordinate liquidity strategy, stabilize stablecoin corridors, and align on bootstrapping and incentive priorities.

The Balancer incentive direction is to primarily drive activity to the Telcoin Network, with an emphasis on stablecoin accessibility. V3 still dominates volume in many aggregator contexts; V4 hook routing limitations have been noted and are being tracked.
