# Newsletter Format — Telcoin Association

## When to use this format

A newsletter is a periodic long-form digest: weekly or monthly. It covers governance activity, protocol developments, and ecosystem progress in a single readable piece. Use it when there is enough accumulated activity to warrant a structured summary — not for individual announcements.

Primary distribution: forum.telcoin.org, email list (when established), X Long-Form thread.

Voice: institutional, factual, no enthusiasm language. Reads as appropriate in a regulatory newsletter. Same brand rules as all @telcoinTAO content.

---

## Structure

**Subject line / title**: Telcoin Association — [Period] Governance & Protocol Update

Each section below is a prose paragraph or two. No bullet points unless listing factual agenda items. No markdown headers in the distribution version (forum, email) — use plain section labels instead.

---

## Template

---

**Telcoin Association**
[Month] [Year] — Governance & Protocol Update

**Governance**

[Summarize council activity from the period. Which councils met, what was decided, what proposals moved to snapshot or ratification. One paragraph per council if there is substantive activity; combine into one paragraph if a council had no notable decisions. Factual only — no interpretation, no editorial takes on decisions.]

Example: The Platform and Treasury Council held its [N]th session on [date]. The council reviewed [topic] and advanced [proposal name] to snapshot vote. Discussion focused on [key issue]. The TELx Council met on [date] and approved [proposal] by [vote count]. Full meeting notes and recordings are available at forum.telcoin.org.

**Protocol**

[Cover any technical progress that is publicly confirmed: testnet status, mainnet sequencing updates, completed milestones from roadmap.telcoin.network. Do not reference internal timelines or unconfirmed dates. One paragraph.]

Example: The Adiri testnet continues hardening through [current phase]. [Specific milestone completed] is confirmed complete. The TEL token upgrade — moving from 2 to 18 decimals with no change to holder balances — is progressing through Council review ahead of formal ratification. Current protocol status is tracked at roadmap.telcoin.network.

**Ecosystem**

[Ecosystem activity attributable to Telcoin Association: developer activity on TAN, TELx liquidity activity, validator participation, community governance participation. One paragraph. No Holdings content, no eXYZ product metrics without confirmation.]

Example: [N] developers [activity]. TELx weekly rewards of 3.2 million TEL were distributed to liquidity miners as scheduled. [Any other confirmable ecosystem data.]

**Looking ahead**

[Next confirmed council meetings, any upcoming governance milestones. No speculative timelines. Link to roadmap for mainnet sequencing.]

Example: Upcoming council sessions: [Council] on [date] at [time] EST. The next TELip/TANIP/TELxIP vote is scheduled for [date if confirmed]. Protocol roadmap: roadmap.telcoin.network.

## Writing rules specific to newsletters

- Every paragraph is 3-5 sentences. No single-sentence paragraphs.
- Section labels are plain text, not markdown headers.
- No conversation prompts — newsletters are informational, not engagement-optimized.
- No emojis.
- Numbers are factual and sourced — if you don't have the number, write around it rather than estimating.
- Do not summarize what the reader is about to read. Start each section directly.
- Passive constructions are acceptable here — "The proposal was ratified" is cleaner than "The Council ratified the proposal" when the council identity is implied from context.
- The newsletter is the complete record. Link to sources (forum, snapshot, YouTube) — don't summarize what is available in full elsewhere.

## Worked example (partial)

---

**Telcoin Association**

**Governance**

The Platform and Treasury Council held its 35th session on July 10. The council reviewed the TEL token upgrade proposal, which moves the token standard from 2 to 18 decimals while preserving all holder balances at a 1:1 exchange rate. The proposal advanced to Council vote with no dissenting positions recorded. The TELx Council met on July 9 and reviewed liquidity distribution metrics from the Trading Fee Rebate Program's second full week of operation. Full recordings of both sessions are available at youtube.com/@TelcoinTAO. Meeting notes are posted at forum.telcoin.org.

**Protocol**

The Adiri testnet is in active hardening. Stress testing across community-run nodes completed successfully in Phase 2, with large burst transaction handling confirmed operational. The TEL token upgrade is in governance review — current holder balances are unaffected until the upgrade completes and the one-for-one exchange window opens. Mainnet sequencing status is tracked at roadmap.telcoin.network.

**Looking ahead**

Upcoming council sessions: Platform and Treasury Council on July 24 at 4:00 PM EST. TELx Council on July 23 at 3:00 PM EST. Community members may observe both sessions via X (@TelcoinTAO) or YouTube (youtube.com/@TelcoinTAO).

## Cadence

- Weekly: appropriate during active mainnet ramp or major governance periods
- Monthly: appropriate during steady-state periods between milestones
- Do not publish a newsletter if there is no substantive governance or protocol activity to report — a thin newsletter damages credibility more than a skipped edition
