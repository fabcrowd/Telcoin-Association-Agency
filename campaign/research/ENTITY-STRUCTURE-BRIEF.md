## The One-Sentence Summary

There are two legally distinct entities: **Telcoin Holdings Pte. Ltd.** (Singapore commercial company) owns the products — the Wallet, the bank, eUSD, the remittance corridors. **Telcoin Association** (Swiss Verein) governs the protocol — Telcoin Network, TELx, TAN, and the TEL treasury.

They share leadership and mission. They are NOT the same entity. @telcoinTAO speaks for the Association only.

## Entity 2 — Telcoin Association

**Type**: Swiss Verein (non-profit association)
**Domicile**: Canton Ticino, Lugano, Switzerland
**Established**: Late 2023 (community ratified via TGIP1)
**Governance**: Elinor Ostrom polycentric model; five specialized councils

### What the Association governs:
- **Telcoin Network** — the L1 blockchain protocol; Platform Council governs validator permissioning and protocol upgrades
- **TELx** — the DeFi liquidity layer; TELx Council sets rules, fee schedules, incentive structures
- **TAN** — the application network; TAN Council governs developer and staker rules
- **TEL token treasury and issuance** — via TELIPs; unanimous Treasury Council approval required
- **MNO validator compliance** — Compliance Council (JD-credentialed members required)

### What the Association does NOT control:
- The Telcoin Wallet
- Telcoin Digital Asset Bank
- eUSD / eXYZ stablecoin issuance
- Remittance corridors
- Exchange listings
- Commercial partnerships

## The Formal Bridge Between TA and Holdings

Two documents define the relationship:

1. **Shared Services Agreement** — allows Holdings employees (including Ryan Neuner, Marketing Associate at Holdings) to render services to the Association with proper documentation. This is why Holdings marketing staff work on @telcoinTAO content.

2. **TELIP "Banking the Internet of Money"** (August 2025, authored by Paul Neuner as CEO of Holdings) — proposed using 5 billion TEL from the Association treasury as collateral to secure equity financing for Holdings' Digital Asset Bank. The fact that this required a formal TELIP confirms they are negotiating parties, not the same entity.

---

## TEL Token — Who Controls It

Originally issued in 2017 by Telcoin Pte. Ltd. (Singapore). When Holdings restructured (2022) and the Association was established (2023), governance of the TEL treasury transferred to the Association's miner community. TEL is now a commons resource governed by the Association's council system — not a Holdings-owned asset.
