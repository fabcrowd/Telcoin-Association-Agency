- **Headline**: Celo's MiniPay (Opera partnership) has onboarded 14 million users across 66+ countries with 400 million+ transactions processed; on-chain governance vote to reallocate 160M CELO to Opera running until March 26, 2026
- **Telcoin angle**: Celo/MiniPay is the most direct competitive reference point for Telcoin's mobile-first financial inclusion narrative.
