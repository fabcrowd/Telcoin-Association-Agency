Collecting · day 2

Collection restarted on verified data

This dashboard previously displayed fourteen days of readings that were generated during development, not collected. They have been withdrawn and quarantined with a full forensic record.

Three signatures confirmed it. Each file's hourly breakdown exceeded its own post count by 24–51%, so no file could be a true census of when posts appeared. Sentiment tracked conversation volume at r = 0.97, a coupling real communities do not show — a busy day is not reliably a positive one. And all fourteen files carried an identical collection timestamp. The cause was a single instruction in the collection script telling it to spread posts evenly across the day whenever a timestamp was missing. That rule is gone, replaced by an explicit unknown bucket.

Nothing is plotted until it is measured. The trend panels below stay empty until fourteen real days accumulate, and the loader now refuses any file not marked as measured.

How to read every number here

MEASURED

Reported by the platform's own analytics for an account we control. Our post impressions, engagements, link clicks.

OBSERVED

Counts visible on a third-party post we actually saw. A sample of the conversation, never a census of it.

DERIVED

Calculated from the two above by a stated formula. Always shown with a range or an approximation mark.

UNAVAILABLE

Known gaps, drawn as gaps. An unknown is never quietly rendered as a zero.

What this can and cannot see

The hard limit
Impressions and reach describe our own posts only. For everyone else's posts we report conversation volume from an observed sample. Link clicks and profile clicks on a third party's post are not obtainable at any price, and are never estimated here.
Sample, not census
Community collection runs on public search, which returns a portion of the conversation rather than all of it. Every community figure carries its sample size. Treat direction and change as meaningful; treat absolute totals as a floor.
Sources
X/Twitter, Reddit, and crypto news outlets for community signal. Our own post performance comes from X native analytics for @telcoinTAO.
Classification
Each post is read and tagged for sentiment and subject against a fixed taxonomy. Subjects the Association does not publish on, such as price, are tracked for listening and marked accordingly — they never generate a recommendation to publish.
Deliberately absent
No single composite score. A blended 0–10 number hides which input moved and cannot be audited, and this page is read by people who will act on it.
