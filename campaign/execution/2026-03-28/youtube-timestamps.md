> **How to use this file**
>
> Each section has chapter titles ready to paste into a YouTube description.

>
> **Fastest way to get time codes (2 minutes per video):**
> 1. Open the video on YouTube
> 2. Click `...` (More) below the video → **Open transcript**
> 3. The transcript panel shows every line with a timestamp
> 4. Scan for the moment each topic starts — note the time
> 5. Paste the filled-in block into the YouTube description
>
> **YouTube chapter rules:**
> - First chapter must be `0:00`
> - Minimum 3 chapters
> - Each chapter must be 10+ seconds apart
> - Format: `0:00 Chapter Title` (one per line, in the description)

**YouTube accepts both formats:**
- `0:00` (minutes:seconds)

For council meetings (typically 60–90 min), use `1:23:45` format for anything past the hour mark.

## Standing Workflow Recommendation

Once YouTube chapters are added to a video:
1. The video shows a chapter bar below the player — viewers skip to what matters
2. Each chapter becomes a searchable title in YouTube search
3. Watch time typically increases — viewers don't abandon at the 10-minute mark
4. Chapter titles from this file can feed directly into tweet thread structures for repurposing

**Add chapters to every council stream within 48 hours of upload** — not just new ones. The backlog of unchaptered recordings on the channel is worth addressing systematically.
